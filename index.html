Why it's different

Every other tool keeps your data.

The difference isn't fancier security — it's where your data gets processed.

Typical SaaS (cloud)

Your data moves to their servers

Your revenue, margins, and customer numbers get sent to and stored on someone else's servers. Once it's there, it's a breach target and a liability — theirs to hold, not yours.
